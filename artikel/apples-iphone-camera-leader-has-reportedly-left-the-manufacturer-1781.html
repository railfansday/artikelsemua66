Industry observers note that jon McCormack, Apple’s vice president of camera software engineering, left the tech giant last month, Bloomberg reports. Its sources say McCormack retired “at the end of October after more than eight years at the company.” Apple reportedly has not yet named a upcoming VP to step in for McCormack, who oversaw its Camera and Photos apps, and the tech that defined generations of photos and the photography and editing experience on Apple’s devices.

McCormack played a major role in defining how iPhone photos look, and he was always ready to get into the weeds with reporters after iPhone events. In 2024, he told The Verge that Apple sees a photo as “a personal celebration of something that really, actually happened.”.

The departure follows John Ternus taking over for Tim Cook as Apple’s CEO in September. Apple has had other major leadership changes recently, including Phil Schiller stepping down as head of the App Store and Apple events and Steve Smith stepping in as the tech giant’s latest M&A and corporate development boss.
