New leaked images of Motorola’s next folding smartphone shared by SammyGuru add more credence to the theory that widescreen could be the default design for foldables moving forward. Following a single leaked shot shared to Reddit yesterday, this new crop of images reveal the Motorola Razr Flex from several angles in two finishes with its inner screen completely open and partially folded.

Sources indicate that the photos show the Razr Flex in two finishes: coriander brown and dark botanical garden, according to SammyGuru. The brown option features a back panel with a bumpy leather-like finish, while the green has a fabric-like woven finish. The cover screen has a hole punch selfie camera centered at the top, while the Razr Flex’s inner screen pushes the camera to the upper right corner. On the back is a pill-shaped bar with two cameras and a flash.

It has been revealed that the Flex is expected to be a smaller and more affordable alternative to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 which has a 5.5-inch cover screen and a 7.6-inch main display when unfolded. But more specific details ,including the size of the Razr Flex, its pricing, and when it’s expected to be debut, are still unknown.
