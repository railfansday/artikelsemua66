Anthropic lets apps bill Claude costs to their customers

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that anthropic has rolled out Linked billing in beta, letting apps built on Claude bill API usage to a customer’s own Claude organisation. Customers can set a spend cap or unlink the app, but only organisations that pay Anthropic directly qualify, and their own data protections do not carry over.

Apps built on Claude can now charge their AI usage to their customers’ own Claude accounts. The feature, called Linked billing, launched in beta on Tuesday, according to Anthropic’s release notes.

Sources indicate that A customer first links its Claude organisation to the app and approves it. The developer then chooses, on each request, whether the customer or the developer pays. The request still runs on the developer’s account, with its usage limits and model access.

Recent findings suggest that customers can set a monthly spending cap for each app in the Claude Console, or unlink it at any time. If Anthropic cannot bill the customer, the request is refused rather than charged to the developer. Anthropic is strict on limits elsewhere too: it ends enterprise discounts once customers use up their tokens.

It has been revealed that not every customer qualifies. It only works for organisations that pay Anthropic directly, not those billed through AWS, Google Cloud or Azure, or with only a Claude subscription. Credits from Claude aims and Anthropic’s startup programmes cannot be used either.

Sources indicate that there is also a privacy catch. A customer’s own data protections, such as zero data retention and HIPAA agreements, do not apply to these requests.
