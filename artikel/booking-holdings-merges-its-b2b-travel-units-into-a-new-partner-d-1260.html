Booking Holdings merges its B2B travel units into a new partner division

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A Booking.com sign in Amsterdam, where the firm is headquartered.

According to recent reports, booking Holdings is putting two of its units that sell travel to other firms into one newly introduced unit, Booking Partner Division, the manufacturer said on Wednesday. The division will start operating in January 2027.

Sources indicate that it brings together Booking.com Strategic Partnerships and Rocket Travel by Agoda. Omri Morgenshtern, chief executive of Agoda, will run it.

According to recent reports, the division sells travel to companies that want to offer trips under their own brands. Its customers include banks, airlines, online travel firms, travel tech sites and publishers.

Partners can use ready-made booking sites under their own name, plug into its systems or sell branded travel deals. They will have access to more than 4.7 million properties, more than 500 airlines and over 200 car rental firms, according to Booking.

Booking is betting the larger unit can drive growth as AI changes how people find and book trips, CNBC reported. Rival Expedia’s own unit for business partners demonstrates how much such a platform can add to profits, CNBC noted.

In a recent development, the company also looks a travel-focused AI agent for partners. Morgenshtern told CNBC that an impressive demo is not enough, and that the agent must work at scale, including during travel emergencies. No launch date has been set.

Morgenshtern put the wider market the division is chasing at about $2tn, according to CNBC. Booking did not give revenue or profit targets for the unit.

“Travel is an increasingly powerful way for businesses to deliver value to their customers and drive growth,” Morgenshtern said in a statement.
