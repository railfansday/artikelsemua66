Today, pre-orders begin for the fresh Intelligent Eyewear collections, made in collaboration with Gentle Monster, Warby Parker, and Samsung. With help from Gemini, these frames are ready to bring you real-time assistance throughout your day, right when you need it. 1.

In a recent development, these collections, which were designed to fit a variety of styles, integrate helpful technology into frames you’ll want to wear every day, and work with Pixel and Samsung phones, as well as iPhones. 2.

According to recent reports, gentle Monster’s high-end designs transform glasses into a means of self-expression. Their Intelligent Eyewear Collection includes six designs in bold colors — including dark red, pink, gray, and yellow — paired with tinted, clear, blue, or brown lenses. Prescription options are available for most frames. Starting at $449, the collection will be available in the United States and in South Korea.

Sources indicate that intelligent eyewear was engineered to be comfortable and helpful all day. The glasses feature lightweight frames (weighing around 41 grams) and a battery that lasts up to nine hours. 3 Each pair comes with a sleek, portable charging case that can recharge your frames up to seven times. 4.

In a recent development, gemini on intelligent eyewear helps connect the dots across the Google apps and services you use every day to get you hands-free help. Just say “Hey Gemini” or tap your frames to learn the story behind a street art mural you are passing by, ask for a tailored recommendation from a “New Arrivals” bookshelf, or look at an event poster to check your Calendar availability on the spot.

Gemini also helps handle your busywork in the background. Whether you’re walking between meetings or running errands with your hands full, you can ask Gemini to add what you’re looking at to your grocery list, or remind you where your next meeting is — all while keeping your eyes up and your day moving.

It has been revealed that when you’re exploring somewhere latest, Google Maps delivers turn-by-turn directions in your ear. And when you ask Gemini, Google Translate will deliver real-time speech translation with automatic language detection across more than 70 languages.

The glasses also feature Gemini’s latest Guided Vision, which offers conversational audio descriptions to people who are blind, have low vision, or want intuitive visual assistance as they read small print or find everyday items. 5 And thanks to our collaboration with Aira, people can say “Hey Gemini, open Aira Explorer” to stream their glasses' camera feed directly to a professionally trained human visual interpreter for real-time support.

Custom-tuned open-ear speakers direct clear vocals and balanced audio right into your ear while keeping you fully aware of what’s around you. You can soundtrack your day with YouTube Music, Spotify, or Apple Music, and jump back into your audiobook on Audible. 6 Or use your glasses to catch up on your missed group chats, respond to messages, and take phone calls hands-free.

With the integrated 12-megapixel ultra-wide camera, capturing high-quality photos and videos is as simple as pressing a button — or just asking Gemini. Your phone stays in your pocket so you can capture the moment while staying in it. HDR processing and image stabilization ensure your images and videos stay crisp, vibrant, and steady, while spatial audio capture helps you relive the moments like you were there.
