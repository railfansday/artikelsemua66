Latest Update: Microsoft puts Word, Excel and PowerPoint inside Copilot at Ignite

🌐 Source: Thenextweb • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (620 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Microsoft Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella speaks at Microsoft Ignite 2026 in San Francisco, 17 November 2026.
  • Microsoft has built Word, Excel and PowerPoint into Copilot.
  • Recent findings suggest that office in Copilot is now generally available, according to Microsoft’s Ignite news page.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Microsoft Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella speaks at Microsoft Ignite 2026 in San Francisco, 17 November 2026.

Microsoft has built Word, Excel and PowerPoint into Copilot. Chief executive Satya Nadella called Copilot “the upcoming operating system for work” at Ignite in San Francisco on Tuesday.

Recent findings suggest that office in Copilot is now generally available, according to Microsoft’s Ignite news page. So is Home, which brings Chat and Cowork together in one place.

Code turns plain language into working apps. Autopilot is an agent that keeps working in the background. Both are rolling out to customers in Microsoft’s Frontier programme.

“But time has come for a true multiplayer form factor,” Nadella stated.

That product is Copilot Workspaces, shared spaces for teams, their agents and Copilot. Nadella also declared a Salesforce plugin for Copilot and fresh plugins for Dynamics 365.

Nadella opened the keynote with last week’s Microsoft 365 outage, which took down Outlook and Teams around the world.

Recent findings suggest that “We know the trust you place in our services and take that responsibility extremely seriously. And in that context, I know we have work to do,” Nadella mentioned.

According to recent reports, microsoft IQ is a newly introduced platform that gathers a company’s data from Fabric, Foundry and Microsoft 365. It adds more than 350 third-party connectors, so agents can work with the full business context.

Agent 365 already manages 50 million agents. It now gains Business Analytics, which shows what drives AI spend and ties it to outcomes.

“You can simply think of this as an ERP system of AI usage,” Nadella mentioned.

Perception, Microsoft’s security system of red, blue and green team agents, is now generally available. The agents attack, detect and fix.

Microsoft also launched the AI Transparency Initiative with 40 organisations. It will build open standards for checking what AI models do, and expand Microsoft’s work in NIST’s Agent Standards Initiative.

“How can you even trust all this powerful technology? And the reality is, by definition, you can’t,” Nadella said.

Sources indicate that microsoft’s own newly introduced models, led by MAI Code 2, were the other big launch of the day. TNW covered them separately, along with Microsoft’s price pitch against Claude.

In a recent development, nadella took questions at a press roundtable after the keynote. He stated he is “AGI-pilled” about how AI could change the economy and politics, he told The Verge.

Asked whether models like Anthropic’s Claude are conscious, he stated: “I don’t think so.” He called it a good debate and said he is not dogmatic about it.

Recent findings suggest that “If the Marxist sort of view is that there’s only one firm, then it’s over, right? For all of us,” Nadella said.

Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia’s chief executive last week. Nadella called him a singular person but noted his return is not his call, Fortune reported. Nvidia’s board has said it does not intend to bring Huang back.

“It’s up to the Nvidia board to decide what happens next,” Nadella remarked.

Nadella told reporters that AI code and AI deployments did not cause the outage. A bug surfaced under specific traffic conditions during routine testing, he stated. Internal AI tools helped engineers fix it faster.

Some communities voted to restrict data centres in the US midterms. Nadella stated support has to be earned one community at a time. The results have not changed Microsoft’s building plans, he added.

“I don’t think the public is getting it wrong,” Nadella said.
