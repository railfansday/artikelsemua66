Amazon Prime Has a New Meal Delivery Perk Just in Time for Black Friday

🌐 Source: Cnet • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (406 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • According to recent reports, amazon’s fresh meal delivery partner will offer free delivery, plus $20 off the first two Uber Eats eligible orders of $25 or more for the first 14 days.
  • The Amazon and Uber Eats partnership is just in time for the holidays if you want convenient dinner option while you shop Black Friday deals and sales.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that one of your Amazon Prime perks made a switch. GrubHub’s out and Uber Eats is in.

According to recent reports, amazon’s fresh meal delivery partner will offer free delivery, plus $20 off the first two Uber Eats eligible orders of $25 or more for the first 14 days. You can expect some Amazon-exclusive offers, and if you have a Prime Visa credit card, you’ll earn 5% back on all Uber Eats orders until Dec. 5 with a Prime membership. The new Uber Eats perk is now available to all Prime members, including those with Prime Access and Prime for Young Adults.

The Amazon and Uber Eats partnership is just in time for the holidays if you want convenient dinner option while you shop Black Friday deals and sales. But heads up, you won’t get the limited-time deal and free delivery if you order through the Uber Eats app. In order to get free delivery and $20 off your order, go to the Uber Eats tab at the top of your Amazon app or Uber Eats on Amazon’s website. Place your order as usual with a participating restaurant but make sure the $20 discount applies at checkout.

Sources indicate that I tried the fresh perk and promo out for dinner. Aside from the meal, the tip, a nearly $10 service fee, tax and the delivery fee all added up to the cost of another meal — which is worth paying attention to when you order.

And don’t forget, our Amazon Prime membership still offers free same-day delivery on perishable grocery orders over $25 when you order from Amazon Grocery.

It has been revealed that previously, your Prime subscription included a GrubHub Plus membership at no additional cost. GrubHub Plus included no delivery fees, lower service fees and 5% back on pickup orders. An Amazon spokesperson remarked in an email to CNET that the GrubHub and Amazon partnership ended. There’s no telling how long Amazon will partner with Uber Eats.

“We continuously evaluate our third-party offers to ensure they provide the best possible value for Prime members,” the spokesperson said. “As part of that ongoing evaluation, we identified an opportunity to deliver an even better restaurant delivery experience.”.

According to recent reports, however, a GrubHub spokesperson stated in an email to CNET that if you have a Prime membership and activated GrubHub Plus, you’ll have the membership until Dec. 31, 2027.
