Earlier this year we introduced Project Genie, an experimental research prototype that lets you generate and explore interactive worlds. Early testers told us they wanted deeper control over what happens inside those worlds, and easier ways to build and share them.

Project Genie brings together our most advanced world models alongside our Gemini models, empowering you to generate, step inside, and shape worlds directly from your browser.

With Genie 4, our most realistic and interactive world model yet, you can now prompt mid-session to change the world you created. Just type a newly introduced prompt while you navigate your world and you can edit your environments in real time. Shift the weather, drop in a character, or redirect the action. For the first time, Genie 4 pairs these visuals with real-time native audio that adapts as the scene unfolds, from ambient soundscapes to character dialogue.

Industry observers note that the fresh Project Genie agent takes creation beyond a single text or image prompt. Now crafting an interactive universe is as effortless as having a conversation. Simply describe your vision in natural language, and the agent orchestrates Google’s suite of multimodal models to craft initial visuals establishing your world’s signature look, paired with custom background scores tailored to the mood.

You can also bring Gemini models into the session as you explore: it observes what is happening on screen, surfaces context-aware suggestion chips for where to go next, or triggers in-world events automatically as your journey unfolds.

Transform personal photos into explorable spaces in “Living Memory,” wander through rich prehistoric habitats grounded in 360-degree panoramas in “Dino Field Guide,” or pit themed objects against one another on a desk with Gemini serving as your real-time referee in “Rock Paper Scissors.”.

Industry observers note that beyond exploring curated worlds, you can also launch your vision with a simple prompt or remix existing gallery experiences alongside the Project Genie agent — tweaking the underlying world structure, soundtrack, or rules to make it uniquely your own. Once you’ve crafted something special, publish your creation to the community gallery for others to explore and remix.

We are committed to developing and rolling out interactive world models responsibly. All video and audio generated in Project Genie includes SynthID watermarking, and all model inputs and outputs go through safety screenings.

In a recent development, genie 3 and Genie 4 remain world models in the frontier research space. As the research continues to evolve, we are actively tackling key challenges such as camera latency, intricate physical interactions, real-time audio precision, and long-term scene permanence.
