Latest Update: Google opens its Genie 4 world model to AI Ultra subscribers

🌐 Source: Thenextweb • 📅 18 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (255 words, 📖 Standard)

  • Google DeepMind first showed Genie 4 to testers in October.

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Genie 4 creates a world you can move through, and lets you change it as you go. Typing a prompt mid-session can shift the weather, add a character or redirect what happens. It also adds sound for the first time, from background noise to character dialogue.

Google DeepMind first showed Genie 4 to testers in October. The newly introduced release adds an AI agent that builds worlds from a conversation, and a gallery where people can share and remix each other’s worlds. Project Genie already lets some users explore simulated cities built from Street View.

The older Genie 3 stays available for longer runs, and now lasts up to three minutes per generation, up from 60 seconds. Substantial game studios remain wary, and Take-Two claims GTA 6 used no generative AI.

Google claims both models are still research systems. Details can drift in long sessions, and readable text and complex physics remain hard. Everything made in Project Genie carries SynthID, Google’s invisible watermark for AI content.

According to recent reports, world models are drawing major money: AMD agreed to buy Fei-Fei Li’s World Labs for $8.2B in September. Google DeepMind claims such models could also train robots and AI agents before they work in the real world. Rival lab Odyssey says its Odyssey-3 model can already control robots and cars.
