In a recent development, we’re adding Google Wallet and Drive to Personal Intelligence, allowing Search to provide answers based on your spending, documents, and chats. These updates help you manage finances, organize projects, and maintain context across your search threads. You can opt in to connect these apps by visiting your Search Personalization settings, where you remain in control of your data and can adjust your preferences at any time.

Robby Stein, VP of Product for Google Search, explains that you can now connect your apps like Google Wallet and Drive to Search for more personalized results. When you opt in, Search uses your own documents, spending habits, and recent activity to provide tailored answers. This helps you track financial goals, organize project information, and continue research tasks without repeating yourself. You remain in control and can manage these connections in your settings at any time.

The most helpful search experience brings together the richness of the world’s information with insights uniquely relevant to you. Today, Search is getting even more tailored with the ability to connect more of your apps to Personal Intelligence — and we're introducing more personally relevant responses, so it's easier to find just what you need.

According to recent reports, starting to roll out today, you can opt in to securely connect Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, with support for Drive and the Gemini app following in the coming weeks — bringing context from your spending, documents, and chats directly to Search. These apps join Gmail, Calendar, and Photos in delivering even more tailored responses with your personal context.

Sources indicate that to connect your apps to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, open Search Personalization, select Connected Apps, and then choose the apps you want to connect. You choose if and when you opt in, and you can turn those connections on or off at any time. You’re always in control of your personalization settings, and soon you’ll also be able to view the context AI Mode uses via the “Personalization” icon below any tailored response. If you want to edit anything simply let AI Mode know what to change, like responding in thread “I’m no longer a vegetarian.”.

Sources indicate that you’ll also start seeing more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode, tailored to your recent search context. So if you’ve been researching and planning for a trip, you can pick up right where you left off in a fresh search thread without starting over or repeating yourself. As always, you can adjust or turn off personalization at any time in your Search Settings.
