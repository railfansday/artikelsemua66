📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq on Thursday, 34% above the $83 IPO price, valuing it at more than $2T, per Business Insider. The manufacturer raised $107.3B with its IPO and a private placement, the largest listing on record.

It has been revealed that anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq on Thursday, 34% above their $83 IPO price, Business Insider reported. The jump, bigger than the 27% grey markets had pointed to, valued the Claude maker at more than $2T. The stock trades under the ticker ANT.

In a recent development, dario and Daniela Amodei rang the opening bell with staff earlier in the day. By midday in New York, the shares had eased to about $107, still up nearly 29%, according to Bloomberg data.

The open beat SpaceX’s debut in June. SpaceX, which priced its IPO at $135 a share, opened 11% higher and closed its first day up 19%.

Recent findings suggest that anthropic raised about $81B by selling shares at $83 on Wednesday, above its $70 to $78 range. It is also raising $26B from a private placement at the same price, with Alphabet, Microsoft and Nvidia among the buyers. Together, that makes $107.3B, the biggest IPO on record.

The debut lifted the wider market, with the Nasdaq Composite up more than 1% after the open. As the first big pure-play AI stock, Anthropic is likely to be watched as a test of investors’ appetite for AI.

Not everyone is convinced. In October, research firm Newly introduced Constructs stated Anthropic might be worth as little as $144B. On Wednesday, a group of scientists signed a statement calling it “not a safe AI manufacturer”.
