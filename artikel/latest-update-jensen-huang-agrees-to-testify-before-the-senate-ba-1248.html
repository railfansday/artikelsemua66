Senator Tim Scott, chair of the Senate Banking Committee, at a White House reception in February 2025.

It has been revealed that former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has agreed to appear before the Senate Banking Committee, its chair, Tim Scott, mentioned on Wednesday. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has also agreed to testify, Scott said on CNBC’s Squawk Box.

It has been revealed that “They both have agreed. We will hopefully have this done before Christmas,” Scott stated.

Scott had asked Huang to appear at a public hearing rather than in a closed-door interview, CNBC reported. He gave no date. Last week he had asked for Huang on 3 December and Lutnick on 10 December.

Sources indicate that A spokesperson for Huang declined to comment on Scott’s announcement, CNBC reported. On Tuesday, Huang agreed to a closed-door interview with three House committees on 1 December.

“More information about what they knew, when they knew it and why they didn’t do something about it,” Scott stated of what the appearances would give the committee.

It has been revealed that he singled out Lutnick over Megaspeed, the Singapore company where the suspected Chinese spy Alice Huang was an executive. US investigators suspect Megaspeed of diverting Nvidia chips to China.

“They were warned as early as March of this year, several months ago, that Megaspeed should be on the ‘entity list’,” Scott remarked.

Industry observers note that the entity list is the Commerce Department’s list of companies the US treats as national security risks. Its Bureau of Industry and Security did not add Megaspeed, Scott mentioned.

“Howard didn’t do it, we want to know why,” Scott mentioned.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has also blamed Commerce for not acting on Megaspeed.

Democrats take control of Congress in January. Senator Mark Kelly, an Arizona Democrat on the Senate Intelligence Committee, said Lutnick “needs to come to Congress”, Semafor reported.

Industry observers note that “We haven’t received answers to the questions that I have and my colleagues have. What we need is answers,” Kelly mentioned of Huang.
