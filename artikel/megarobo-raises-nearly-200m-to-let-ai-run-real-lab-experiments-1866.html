MegaRobo raises nearly $200M to let AI run real lab experiments

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that chinese lab automation company MegaRobo Technologies has raised nearly $200M in a round joined by the Oman Investment Authority and CMB International. It builds systems that let AI design experiments, run them in robotic labs and learn from the results, and intends to expand across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

China’s MegaRobo Technologies has raised nearly $200M to build labs where AI can run its own experiments, the company confirmed on Thursday. The Oman Investment Authority, the country’s sovereign wealth fund, joined the round, along with new investor CMB International.

Existing backers Asia Investment Capital, Granite Asia and Sinovation Ventures also put in more money. MegaRobo did not disclose its valuation.

According to recent reports, AI can read papers and predict molecules, but science still needs real tests. MegaRobo links AI models to robots and lab tools. A model can proposal a test, run it and use the results to pick the next one.

“That shift changes AI from a system that interprets the world into one that can help create latest knowledge, materials, processes and products,” stated founder and CEO Daniel Huang.

Recent findings suggest that megaRobo claims it has more than 500 customers, mostly drug makers, biotech firms and research labs. Its Megalaxy Lab lets scientists and AI agents send tests to robot labs, and has served more than 200 of them.

Sources indicate that it has also released Poreva Open, a protein design contest with Tencent Cloud and DNA maker Twist Bioscience. Megalaxy Lab will test more than 10,000 AI-designed proteins in a real lab, to set a shared benchmark built on actual results. Anthropic, for one, had outside labs check Claude’s protein designs in August.

In a recent development, money is pouring into AI drug design. Alphabet’s Isomorphic Labs raised $5B on Tuesday, and Chai Discovery raised $400M in July. In Berlin, Dunia Innovations is building a €280M robotic lab to test what AI designs.

According to recent reports, megaRobo says it has raised about $600M in total, including a $300M Series C backed by Goldman Sachs in 2022. It prepares to expand across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia, and to use the same technology beyond science, much like Jeff Bezos’ physical AI lab.
