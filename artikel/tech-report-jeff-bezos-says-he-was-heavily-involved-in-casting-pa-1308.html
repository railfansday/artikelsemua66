Tech Report: Jeff Bezos says he was ‘heavily involved’ in casting Pattinson as Bond

Amazon founder Jeff Bezos at Blue Origin in Cape Canaveral, Florida, in February 2026.

It has been revealed that jeff Bezos remarked on Tuesday he was “heavily involved” in casting Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond. He told Semafor’s Liz Hoffman that five finalists did full screen tests. Once the team watched them, the choice was clear.

“By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing. But there was something special about Rob,” Bezos noted.

Bezos spoke at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World conference. Vik Bajaj, his co-founder at the AI start-up Prometheus, joined him on stage. He also gave the Nvidia board advice on replacing Jensen Huang and shared new figures on Prometheus.

Recent findings suggest that amazon MGM Studios named Pattinson as the newly introduced Bond on Monday. Amazon bought MGM in 2022, and this is the first Bond casting under its control.

According to recent reports, the team cut a large shortlist down to five finalists, Bezos noted. Each did “real old-fashioned screen tests” of three scenes, in full costume, on real sets and with other actors. The group, director Denis Villeneuve among them, spent a couple of hours on Friday reviewing the tests. They made the final call that day.

Industry observers note that at first the team leaned slightly against Pattinson because of his fame, Bezos said. They weighed picking someone less famous.

“But in the screening tests, it was so obvious he is James Bond,” Bezos said.

Sources indicate that asked if Huang should return to Nvidia, Bezos noted he did not know what happened in the board meetings. Huang said on Tuesday he would come back if asked. The board has ruled out his return.

It has been revealed that “If you don’t bring Jensen back, I would not hire an outsider. Nvidia is too complicated,” Bezos remarked.

Outside CEOs in complex companies have a “vanishingly small chance of succeeding”, he added. Hiring one should be a last resort. Amazon’s own handover is no guide, he stated, because it followed a initiative that ran for years. Huang, he said, deserves admiration and respect “no matter what”.

Industry observers note that bezos mentioned his AI start-up Prometheus has about 250 staff and has raised roughly $14.5bn. Prometheus has already put much of that into computing power. The organization builds AI models for engineering and manufacturing, which he called “AI for atoms”.

In a recent development, its tools have designed analogue chips and radios in 12 hours, he noted. That work would take a team of 10 to 20 people six to nine months. Blue Origin, his space company, is testing the tools on two projects in a private trial with early customers. He gave no date for a public launch.

Elon Musk is merging his companies. Asked if he would do the same with Prometheus, Blue Origin and Amazon, Bezos called it a funny idea. He noted he had “nothing to announce”.

Recent findings suggest that he also defended his contacts with President Donald Trump, who he stated “reaches out a lot”. Shutting business leaders out is a mistake, he said. Politicians who blame Major Tech for high living costs are picking a villain, he added. They should fix the root cause instead.
