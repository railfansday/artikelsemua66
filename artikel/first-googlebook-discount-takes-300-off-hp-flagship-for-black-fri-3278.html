It has been revealed that the first discount on Googlebooks following the October 4 launch is here. Best Buy is taking $300 off the HP Googlebook 14 for Black Friday.

In a recent development, the HP Googlebook 14 goes from $1,299 to $999 with this Black Friday Deal. It has a 14-inch 2.8K OLED touchscreen and is powered by the Snapdragon X Elite (like the Dell XPS) with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage. Premium features include a 19-hour battery life, haptic touchpad, and face unlock to complement the fingerprint sensor. There are two USB-C ports and a headphone jack.

The only other device with face unlock is the Lenovo Googlebook 15, which is $100 off at Best Buy to $999. At 15.3 inches (2.8K OLED touchscreen), this is the largest Googlebook. It’s powered by the Intel Core Ultra 5 with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage. Battery life comes in at 13 hours, while there are two USB-C ports, USB-A, HDMI, and 3.5mm jack.
