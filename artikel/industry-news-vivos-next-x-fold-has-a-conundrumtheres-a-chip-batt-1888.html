Industry News: Vivo's next X Fold has a conundrum—there's a chip battle, and only one will win

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Vivo is working on another foldable after debuting the X Fold 6 this past summer. Rumors suggest the Chinese OEM faces a decision: which chip to use.

Industry observers note that this week, rumors on Weibo from tipster Digital Chat Station (DCS) suggest that Vivo's early X Fold 7 development has reached an notable junction (via GSMArena). Here's where things get interesting—competition is the key ingredient. DCS suggests that Vivo's next foldable has been progressing well; however, the device is being tested with two chips in mind. These chips are from MediaTek and Qualcomm.

It has been revealed that DCS claims Vivo is testing the Fold 7 with MediaTek's Dimensity 9600 Pro and Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite/Elite Extreme. Remember that the Elite Extreme is geared more toward "Ultra-level" phones, but that's neither here nor there. The tipster adds that Vivo might not consider specs and power for its X Fold 7 SoC.

Instead, the Chinese OEM could look at which company offers the most "competitive pricing." The tipster mentions one other aspect of this phone: its screen ratio.

It has been revealed that DCS claims Vivo is moving forward with a "standard screen ratio" in mind. For comparison, imagine the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra—that's the general feel.

Sources indicate that funnily enough, we've broken down these two chips from MediaTek and Qualcomm and put them head-to-head. Both chips utilize the latest 2nm process, and they're pretty darn similar—two sides of the same coin, if you will. Each chip's CPU capabilities are impressive. For the GPU, both Qualcomm and MediaTek have made strides in improving the graphics performance mobile devices can achieve. This should be a boon to gamers looking to play Wuthering Waves or NTE or another intensive game.

Sources indicate that with that in mind, it feels like Vivo is spoiled for choice (it kind of is). The X Fold 6 released this summer with MediaTek's Dimensity 9500 Super Edition. Before that, it settled with Qualcomm's Snapdragon.
