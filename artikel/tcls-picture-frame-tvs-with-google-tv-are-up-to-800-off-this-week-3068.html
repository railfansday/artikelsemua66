Get a gorgeous newly introduced TCL Designer Series Picture Frame TV with Google TV built-in for as low as $649.99 during the tech giant’s Black Friday week sales.

Recent findings suggest that if you’re in the market for a new TV like I am, Black Friday is always the best time of year to do it. TCL understood the assignment and has heavily discounted its newest Picture Frame TVs during Black Friday week, all of which not only look gorgeous on your wall, but have superb picture quality and awesome software thanks to Google TV built-in.

Sources indicate that the best deal of the sale is the $800-off 75-inch 75A300L, which uses a 144Hz QLED panel that supports Dolby Vision and other HDR standards. These TVs are just 1.1 inches thin and come with a brilliant flush mount that helps complete the illusion of it just being a picture frame hung on your wall. The 2017 LG OLED currently hanging on my wall sits four entire inches from the wall because of how large the component portion is.

Recent findings suggest that the other main option is the higher-end 400U series of TCL QD-Mini LED Frame TVs, which use a different type of LED technology, but are still 144Hz. The 400U series packs a peak HDR brightness of 2500 nits, which is almost certainly brighter than whatever TV you’re looking to replace it with. My 2017 LG OLED I referenced earlier can only hit 800 nits, for example.

Sources indicate that no matter which model or size you pick, all TCL Frame TVs come with Google TV, which is easily the best TV operating system today. Samsung’s The Frame TVs look similar, but then you have to deal with their awful TV OS, and I wouldn’t want to wish that on anyone. Google TV is clean, has an easy-to-use interface, features Gemini built in for fast and easy searches, and works perfectly with your Android phones and Google Home-powered smart home.

Plus, the matte display helps substantially reduce reflections and interference from other light sources in your room, ensuring your Frame TV’s image looks as good as the gorgeous picture frame that surrounds it.
