The iconic YouTube app icon that looked like a retro TV found on iOS might be making a comeback, with newly introduced customizable icons showing up behind the scenes of a new YouTube upgrade.

In a recent development, in the early days of the iPhone, Apple built a custom app for YouTube on iOS before Google built its own. That included an app icon that looked like a retro TV and it was later replaced when YouTube’s official app debuted on the platform.

Now, that icon might be coming back.

Android Authority spotted several upcoming app icons YouTube for Android is testing in its latest beta patch (v21.47.483-BETA). These icons seem to be tied to a new feature that would allow consumers to customize the homescreen icon for YouTube, and includes seven designs in total including two throwback designs. One is the aforementioned retro TV icon used on iOS, which was never on YouTube for Android, and another is an old stacked YouTube logo design.

That said, it’s very fun to get these throwbacks, and we’ll be excited to see this functionality go live.
