🌐 Source: Blog • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (105 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • We’re partnering with Blue Origin on the first heavy-lift launch for Project Suncatcher, our long-term moonshot to scale AI infrastructure in space by tapping into the abundant solar power in orbit.
  • Targeted for Q1 2029, Blue Origin’s heavy-lift Fresh Glenn rocket will deploy our satellites — each with roughly the capacity of a data center rack — into sun-synchronous orbit.
  • This dedicated launch builds on our ongoing work with other satellite and launch partners and represents a crucial milestone on our long-term roadmap as we expand the boundaries of what's possible for the future of compute.

Senior Director, Paradigms of Intelligence.

We’re partnering with Blue Origin on the first heavy-lift launch for Project Suncatcher, our long-term moonshot to scale AI infrastructure in space by tapping into the abundant solar power in orbit.

Targeted for Q1 2029, Blue Origin’s heavy-lift Fresh Glenn rocket will deploy our satellites — each with roughly the capacity of a data center rack — into sun-synchronous orbit.

This dedicated launch builds on our ongoing work with other satellite and launch partners and represents a crucial milestone on our long-term roadmap as we expand the boundaries of what's possible for the future of compute.
