📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Every’s graphic announcing its $17.5M Series A.

In a recent development, every, a media organization that teaches professionals and teams to work with AI, has raised a $17.5M Series A at a $110M valuation, it announced on Wednesday. IA Ventures led the round, with Betaworks and The Atlantic chief executive Nicholas Thompson also investing.

The money will go into one subscription for people and teams putting AI to work. Every says its own staff test new AI models on real work every day, then turn what they learn into articles and tools. AI companies are paying for that kind of help too: OpenAI, Anthropic and others back a US worker retraining push that has raised over $500M.

Co-founder and chief executive Dan Shipper admits venture-backed media has a poor record. He indicates he keeps full control of the company, and that IA Ventures partner Jesse Beyroutey, who led the deal, is a close friend.

“The narrative is ‘it’s so over for humans’, and I think that’s exactly wrong,” Shipper told Fortune.

In a recent development, demand for help with AI at work is growing. London’s Multiverse raised $70M in May to sell AI training across Europe. Significant employers are still working it out: Meta planned to cut some teams by up to 60% and hand their work to AI, then cancelled a second round of cuts.
