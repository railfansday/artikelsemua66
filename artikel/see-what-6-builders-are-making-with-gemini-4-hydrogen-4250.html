According to recent reports, we recently introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, bringing cutting-edge intelligence to everyone. Built for rapid execution across diverse agentic workflows, Hydrogen can stand on its own as a model for everyday work — or pair with Carbon as the high-efficiency execution engine in multi-model workflows. Thanks to these capabilities, builders have already been putting Hydrogen to work. Check out six projects showing what Hydrogen can do.

@kimmonismus created a sci-fi-inspired journey through a Three.js skyline with Hydrogen on high thinking in Google Antigravity.

@LuminaBench swept across the sea by plane, showing the passage of time as the scene changes from morning to night.

@IamEmily2050 used Hydrogen in the Gemini app to create a 3D voxel koi pond, complete with a red pagoda, cherry blossoms, and lantern-lit bridges.

Speaking of koi ponds, @notjazii used Hydrogen to put a softer spin on a koi pond with a squishy, jelly-like form instead of angular edges.

It has been revealed that @thtbee_ brought a crimson moth to life pairing our two latest models in Google Antigravity. Gemini 4 Carbon orchestrated a /swarm of Hydrogen workers to create an interactive animation with atmospheric visuals and sound. Then Hydrogen recorded the demo video, too.

And @Bhavani_00007 gave us a look at the progression from Gemini 3.8 Flash to Hydrogen by running the same animated 3D desert ranch prompt on both, with Hydrogen bringing the scene to life in <1 minute.
