Global Lead, Envisioning Studio.

Every culture has its own visual language, and fresh tools are helping more storytellers share theirs with the world. We partnered with Women in Film India (WIF India) to launch the WIF India × Google Flow Filmathon, designed to democratize AI filmmaking and elevate talent beyond traditional production centers across India.

Industry observers note that using Google Flow, our AI creative studio, participants paired their creative visions with AI to build original worlds from scratch. Five finalist short films recently debuted at the International Film Festival of India in Goa, bringing fresh, creative voices from across the country to the festival screen. The winners received their awards from the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Goa Dr. Pramod Sawant.

Click through the carousel below to watch all five short films made with Google Flow and experience a bold latest wave of independent storytelling.

Haunted by visions of his late sister, a brother confronts grief and guilt in “Sikkal,” a chilling folk horror by Promita Banerjee, which took first place in the Filmathon.

A stranger digging a mysterious hole sparks wild rumors across a quiet village in “Kuli,” a sharp Tamil black comedy by Syam Manohar, which came in second place.

In a recent development, in “Pedals of Joy,” a heartfelt animation by Nikhil Shinde, an older brother in Mumbai sets out to fulfill a young boy’s dream. This film took third place.

Aswathi Naduthodi’s visual essay “Bombay Mumbai - Vada Pav,” traces a city’s evolution through the everyday lives connected by one of its most popular street foods.

In Srishti Chaudhary’s dialogue-free animation “Nazar,” a schoolgirl confronts the shadow of the unwanted gaze — and reclaims her sense of hope and power.
