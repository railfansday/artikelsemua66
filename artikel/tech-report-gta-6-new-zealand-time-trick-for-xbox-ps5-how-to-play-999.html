Tech Report: GTA 6 New Zealand time trick for Xbox, PS5: How to play hours early

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, GTA fans have waited 13 years for Grand Theft Auto VI. Finally, the next installment of the GTA series comes out on Nov. 19.

Sources indicate that there's no early access for GTA 6. In theory, you have to wait until the release date. But what if you could play GTA 6 on Nov. 18? Or, more accurately, roughly 18 hours before everyone else in the U.S.? Well, there's a way to do just that.

Sources indicate that GTA 6 will have a global release date, dropping at midnight for each time zone. This means that Newly introduced Zealand, which is 18 hours ahead of Eastern Time in the U.S., will get to play the long-awaited game before everyone else.

If you have an Xbox, there's long been a not-so-secret trick on Xbox consoles that lets U.S.-based gamers access games a little bit before everyone else in the country.

As our friends at PCMag point out, gamers can simply go to their Xbox dashboard and do the following:.

Select Language & Location on the right-hand side.

Change your region to New Zealand.

Hit Restart Now to restart your console and save the settings.

In a recent development, this has previously worked to access other games early, as it tricks the console into thinking it's on Fresh Zealand time. It's possible that Microsoft switches things up and closes the loophole before the launch of GTA 6. But, as of now, unless anything changes, it should work.

This is a quick and easy process, and you'll be able to change the settings back on your Xbox the same way.

Also, be sure to preorder the digital version of GTA 6 as you'll be able to download the game on Nov. 10. While it won't be playable until the release, it'll be ready to go on your console as soon as it does release without having to wait for the game to download and install then.

In a recent development, playStation 5 users, unfortunately, don't have it so simple. And there's no guarantee that this will work either. So, before we go any further, know that it just might not be worth the time and effort.

PlayStation accounts are region-locked, so Sony knows exactly where you're based when you're signed into your account.

According to recent reports, if you want to attempt to access GTA 6 early on your PS5, create a new PSN account while using a VPN (we recommend ExpressVPN) with a New Zealand-based location. From there, you'll actually have to purchase the game under this new account in the New Zealand PlayStation Store. As PCMag points out, this might even cost more due to currency conversions.

And, again, there is no guarantee that this will work.

Worst case, you have to wait another 18 hours before you can play a game you've already waited 13 years for.
