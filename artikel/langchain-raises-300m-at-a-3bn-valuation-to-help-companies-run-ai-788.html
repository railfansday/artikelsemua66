LangChain raises $300m at a $3bn valuation to help companies run AI agents

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

LangChain, which makes tools for building and running AI agents, has raised $300m at a $3bn valuation, the firm announced on Friday. The Series C brings its total funding to $460m.

Industry observers note that capitalG, Alphabet’s independent growth fund and an existing investor, led the round. New investors GIC and DFJ Growth and existing investors IVP and Sapphire Ventures also took part. Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight and MongoDB Ventures joined as new investors. Sequoia, Amplify and Benchmark put in more.

The valuation is more than double the $1.25bn of about a year ago. LangChain then raised $125m in a round led by IVP, TechCrunch reported.

LangChain began in 2022 as an open-source project. The money will go into LangSmith, its platform for running, testing and governing AI agents. It works whatever framework or model a manufacturer uses.

Recent findings suggest that the company notes more than 7,000 organisations use LangSmith. They range from startups such as legal AI firm Harvey, Rippling and Listen Labs to carmaker Toyota, Nvidia and Salesforce. Workday uses it to run and govern agents. That cut Workday’s time to deploy a new agent from five months to under a day, LangChain mentioned.

“Every organization should be free to choose the best models for its needs, control its data and infrastructure, and continuously improve its agents,” noted Harrison Chase, LangChain’s co-founder and CEO.

Chase told the Journal that LangChain’s products aim to make agents’ behaviour easier to control.

“The agent’s behavior is the hard part to control,” Chase noted.

LangChain has 435 full-time staff and intends to grow to 850 by the end of next year, the Journal reported. It also has 47 forward deployed engineers and 13 deployed architects, who work directly with customers. It will hire engineers, researchers, marketers and salespeople with the newly introduced money.

“It’s an insanely competitive market,” Mo Jomaa, a partner at CapitalG, told the Journal.

LangChain says it will show latest LangSmith features at a release event on 20 November.
