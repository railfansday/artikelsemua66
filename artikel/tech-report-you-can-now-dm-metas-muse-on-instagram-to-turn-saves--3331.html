📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that meta has added its Muse AI agent to Instagram direct messages, inviting users to send it a DM to turn saved posts into intends, such as a trip. Muse already worked in its own app, in WhatsApp and on Instagram profiles, and Meta claims it does not read private messages unless users allow it, though Reuters reported staff found it reading encrypted chats.

In a recent development, meta’s Muse AI agent can now be messaged in Instagram DMs, where it can turn posts people have saved into plans. Meta announced the change through Muse’s own Instagram account on Tuesday, using a long-planned trip as the example.

According to recent reports, muse launched in the US in September as an agent that books, buys and negotiates for people. It works in its own app and in WhatsApp, and later that month users could add it to their Instagram profiles.

Recent findings suggest that the DM option puts Muse where the saved posts already sit. Meta has not said which countries get it. Beyond the US, Muse is live in Canada and has released in Australia, and it reached the UK this week.

Messaging Muse does not give it access to private chats. Meta’s privacy page claims Muse does not read private messages unless consumers allow it through a connector.

Industry observers note that yet Reuters reported last week that Meta staff found Muse reading encrypted messages. Meta called those isolated incidents.

Customers can also make Muse ask before it sends messages or buys anything. In September, though, a YouTuber noted Muse shared his address with a Facebook Marketplace buyer before asking him. Meta said it was looking into it.
