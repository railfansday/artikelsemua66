Suno adds AI video to its music app, with filters for artists’ faces

🌐 Source: Thenextweb • 📅 17 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (249 words, 📖 Standard)

  • Visuals runs on its own credits, bought separately from song credits.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

AI music company Suno has launched Visuals, a tool that makes images and short videos to go with people’ songs, it declared on Tuesday. Users can create lyric videos, album art and clips of up to 15 seconds. It is on the web now, with iOS and Android apps due in the next few weeks.

Suno notes its video model is built around music. Clips follow a song’s structure, pick up on its lyrics and lip-sync to them. People can start from Suno’s own characters or upload an image, then extend and edit clips with text prompts.

Visuals runs on its own credits, bought separately from song credits. It follows Suno’s Speech tool, released in October for spoken audio such as poems and meditations.

“This means that prompts that work on other video platforms may not work on Suno and that’s by design,” the tech giant wrote.

It has been revealed that those guardrails come as Suno fights fresh legal battles. Last week, Elvis Presley’s music owners and Eminem’s publisher sued Suno, and the Presley suit claims it misused his voice.

Suno has been trying to look more legitimate. It launched music models trained on licensed songs from Warner and BMG in September, and plans audio watermarks for its tracks. Investors valued the manufacturer at $5.4B in June.
