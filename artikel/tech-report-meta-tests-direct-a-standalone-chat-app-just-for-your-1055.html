Tech Report: Meta Tests Direct, a Standalone Chat App Just for Your Instagram DMs

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that meta is testing a fresh texting app, giving your Instagram DMs a dedicated place away from the app’s feed and stories. The Direct app, announced on Monday, is at the moment being tested in Turkey and Thailand. There’s no date yet on when it might expand to other countries.

According to recent reports, available on both iOS and Android, the Direct app largely mirrors Instagram’s texting feature. It sounds similar to Meta’s Messenger app, which itself spun out of Facebook for texts and video calls. And it would also join WhatsApp, so the tech giant certainly has no shortage of different ways to text.

The Direct app includes several features for personalizing your conversations, including drawing, custom stickers, voice effects and chat background themes. You can also use it with or without an Instagram account, which could be particularly useful for someone who wants to text someone on the platform but intentionally doesn’t want a profile on the service. When using the service without a full Instagram account, you can add others by username or by scanning a QR code.

Across Meta’s chat apps, the firm has taken different approaches to end-to-end encryption and privacy. Instagram’s conversations aren’t currently encrypted, whereas those on Messenger and WhatsApp are.

Meta notes the Direct app will require users to verify their age and will use technology to block anyone under 13 from using the chat app. The service will also provide similar protections for teen accounts as are presently offered in the main Instagram app.
