Trump’s accounts traded up to $97.5M in Nvidia stock, CNBC finds

Jensen Huang, then Nvidia’s CEO, speaks as President Donald Trump looks on at an ‘Investing in America’ event in the Cross Hall of the White House, 30 April 2025.

Sources indicate that president Donald Trump’s accounts have bought or sold Nvidia shares at least 103 times since January 2025, a CNBC analysis found. Luke Fountain’s review of the financial disclosures, updated through August, values the trades at $27M to $97.5M in total.

According to recent reports, the filings give dollar ranges rather than share counts. By the end of 2025, Nvidia was one of Trump’s three largest individual stock holdings, worth $12.5M to $58M. A year earlier, the stake was worth $615,000 to $1.3M.

One sale, of $500,001 to $1M, came on 18 May. That was the first Monday after Trump returned from his state visit to China, which Jensen Huang joined. Five days earlier, Secretary of State Marco Rubio had briefed Trump on intelligence about Huang’s relationship with a suspected Chinese spy, The Upcoming York Times reported.

The White House disputed the Times report without elaborating, the paper noted. Trump denied being briefed when asked about it on 16 November.

“I haven’t heard that,” Trump stated, according to CNBC.

Industry observers note that he added that he did not know whether the woman, Alice Huang, was a Chinese spy. His accounts also bought smaller amounts of Nvidia stock in the days before and after the May sale. Trump disclosed more than 21,000 trades in 2025, CNBC found, against about 500 in his whole first term.

Recent findings suggest that CNBC set Trump’s Nvidia trades against Huang’s growing access. The two have met or appeared together at least 15 times since Trump returned to office, by CNBC’s count. They first met at the White House on 31 January 2025. Two days earlier, Trump’s money managers bought $1M to $5M of Nvidia stock.

It has been revealed that in August 2025, Trump confirmed a deal giving the US government 15% of Nvidia’s revenue from chip sales to China. A week later, his accounts bought $5M to $25M of Nvidia shares.

According to recent reports, on 8 December, Trump remarked Nvidia could sell its more powerful H200 chips in China. His accounts had sold up to $1M of Nvidia stock four days earlier, and bought more the day after.

According to recent reports, from January to August 2026, the accounts made 38 more Nvidia trades, worth $9.4M to $28.3M. On 8 October, Trump gave Huang the National Medal of Science. A disclosure released that day indicated August purchases of up to $1.6M and sales of up to $1M.

A White House spokesperson told CNBC that independent advisers manage Trump’s portfolio without his input.

The arrangement aims to “avoid even the appearance of a conflict of interest,” Trump Organization spokesperson Kimberly Benza told CNBC.

Recent findings suggest that CNBC noted that the disclosures offer no evidence that Trump directed the trades. Nor do they show that his advisers had access to classified intelligence. TNW has not independently verified the analysis.

In a recent development, huang resigned as Nvidia’s CEO on 12 November. Trump has since backed his return, which Nvidia’s board is reportedly not considering.
