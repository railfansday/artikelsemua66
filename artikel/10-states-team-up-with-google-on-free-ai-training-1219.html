Recent findings suggest that we are partnering with ten states to provide free AI training to residents through local libraries, colleges, and workforce agencies. These programs help you gain practical skills for creating marketing materials, analyzing data, and building applications. You can visit grow.google/gov to find available courses and start building your professional capabilities today.

Today, seven in 10 managers believe an AI-trained workforce is critical to their organization’s success, yet they aren’t offering the practical training that would help. We want to make it easy for everyone to learn how to use technology effectively and responsibly when it’s convenient for them, wherever they live and work.

Sources indicate that that’s what led Grow with Google to partner with states across the country to bring no-cost training directly to residents. We started in Oklahoma. Then, we partnered with Massachusetts, where nearly 40,000 residents signed up through the MassTech AI Hub within six months of launch.

North Carolina is now our tenth state partner to offer our digital and AI training at no cost to residents through NC LIVE. It joins Arkansas, Connecticut, Delaware, Georgia, Massachusetts, Missouri, Oklahoma, South Dakota and Virginia.

While each partnership shapes the rollout to fit their communities, they share the common goal of making digital and AI training accessible to everyone.

We hear from people across career fields that they want to know more about AI, but they tell us it can be intimidating to get started.

We created the Google AI Professional Certificate to help them succeed. Through this training course they learn how to use AI to create marketing materials, gain insights from their data, prepare presentations, build their own apps and much more. And they practice hands-on with real examples.
