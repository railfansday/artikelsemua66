After three weeks and over 451,000 folds in our custom Fold-A-Phone machine, Apple’s iPhone Duo is showing its first signs of wearing out its hinge. We’ve been endurance testing Apple’s $1,999 foldable phone since Oct. 23, with the intention of seeing how far the Duo’s first-gen hinge can hold up. But as of Friday, the Duo is beginning to have problems with fully opening in our machine.

CNET Lab Director Matthew Murray will go over these changes on today’s 1 p.m. ET livestream on our YouTube channel, which you can watch embedded above. Our robust in-house machine has been opening and closing the foldable phone around 20,000 times per day to simulate years of wear and tear on the hinge from our CNET Labs location in Louisville, Kentucky.

The livestream isn’t just for watching a phone open and close — you’ll also be able to chat with CNET editors who will be available to answer your iPhone Duo questions live in the chat.
