Sources indicate that during our last six-week Flow Sessions, artists explored our AI creative studio, Google Flow. They developed visual projects, ranging from a pitch for a television series, animated shorts, and personal narratives, while experimenting with fresh workflows to see how our latest models can support their unique styles.

In addition to the visual projects, for Flow Music Sessions, we brought together musicians and producers to take a similar approach to experimenting with latest workflows and tools. They focused on Spaces, a feature within Google Flow Music, to create bespoke audio tools, plugins, mini music apps, new instruments, and digital artifacts that can be used directly in any creative workflow.

Check out the Spaces they created to get inspired, and you can try remixing their work to see how this approach can work for you:.

Bloom Cycles: A vocal plugin that uses your webcam to shift your voice in real time based on your hand gestures.

Track Arranger: A songwriting tool that helps turn short loops into a full song structure with verses, choruses, and drops.

Mel’s Dreamscape Vocals: A bright, pop-focused vocal processor packed with pro presets and easy tuning tools.

Marble and Metal Visualisers: A 3D interactive visualizer that creates custom graphics reacting dynamically to live audio or uploaded music.

Tune Sampler: A vintage-style console plugin inspired by 90s hip-hop gear designed to shape audio with analog warmth.

Mirage Sampler: A playful software instrument that lets you easily drag and drop audio samples to flip them into unique textures and instruments.
